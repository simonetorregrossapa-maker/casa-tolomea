Frequently asked questions

How does booking work? Do I have to pay online?

No online payment. You send us your dates and we reply within 24 hours. On confirmation you pay a 30% deposit by bank transfer; the balance is paid on arrival.

What's included in the price?

The price is for the whole house and all-inclusive: final cleaning, tourist tax, linen, Wi-Fi and air conditioning. No extra costs and no security deposit.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, pets are welcome, free of charge. Just let us know in your request so we can prepare everything.

Is Casa Tolomea open in autumn and winter?

Yes, all year round. From October to March it is a quiet base for Palermo, 20 minutes from the city centre, with free parking outside the house.
